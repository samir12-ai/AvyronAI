Questions, answered.

How is Avyron different from ChatGPT?+

ChatGPT answers from general knowledge. Avyron collects evidence about your specific market, your competitors and your customers, builds the strategy from it, links each claim to its source, and keeps watching for changes.

Do I need a marketing team to use it?+

No. Avyron is built for businesses that don't have one. It tells you what to do each day in plain language. If you do have a team, it gives them a clear strategy to execute.

Where does the data come from?+

Public sources: competitor websites, reviews, social media and online communities. When a source is missing or limited, Avyron says so instead of guessing.

Which businesses is it for?+

Founders, growing businesses and agencies, from local brands to B2B software companies, especially those competing against bigger players.

Does it spend my ad budget automatically?+

No. Avyron recommends where to spend and why. Nothing is spent and no strategy changes without your approval.

What counts as a campaign?+

One full strategy for one product or offer: market analysis, customer voice, positioning, offer, buyer journey and channels. Watchtower and the Performance Loop keep it up to date for the month.
