TASK — AUTHORITATIVE PAIN CLASSIFICATION + CONTEXTUAL DOMINANCE + NO-PAIN-MERGING ENFORCEMENT

We identified a foundational architecture problem in the Audience → Strategy pipeline.

Current risk:

Raw negative signals can become "pains" without enough semantic classification.

Then downstream engines may:

- treat a post-purchase complaint as a purchase motivation
- let refund/support issues hijack positioning or the Offer
- independently reinterpret the same pain
- select different meanings for the same evidence
- merge several unrelated pains into one artificial statement merely to satisfy alignment/compliance guards
- discard useful secondary pains because only one "primary pain" is propagated

We need to replace this behavior with an authoritative classified Pain Registry and CONTEXTUAL DOMINANCE.

CORE PRINCIPLE:

DO NOT choose one pain for the entire strategy.

DO NOT merge all pains together.

Instead:

CLASSIFY → VERIFY → RANK → ROUTE → ENFORCE.

Every pain keeps its own identity and ID.

Each engine selects the highest-priority ELIGIBLE pain for that engine's specific strategic responsibility.

Example:

A purchase motivation such as:

"Businesses are overwhelmed by fragmented marketing tools and lack strategic direction"

may dominate:

Positioning
Differentiation
Mechanism
Offer

while:

"Fear of paying for unclear ROI"

may be used by:

Offer
Funnel
Persuasion
Risk Reversal

and:

"Refund delays / poor support"

may be used by:

Retention
Customer Experience
Trust Safeguards

Refund friction must NOT become core positioning merely because it has strong negative evidence.

PHASE 2 — AUTHORITATIVE PAIN CLASSIFICATION
===========================================================

Every meaningful Audience pain/signal must receive a stable authoritative identity.

Conceptually:

PainSignal {
  id
  statement
  normalizedStatement

  classification
  strategicRole

  evidenceIds[]
  sourceTypes[]

  evidenceStrength
  sourceDiversity
  prevalence

  productFit

  allowedUses[]
  prohibitedUses[]

  eligibleForCoreStrategy

  rootCauseIds[]
  signalRootIds[]

  classificationReason
  classifierVersion
}

Adapt this to existing schemas rather than blindly creating this exact interface.

Required semantic classifications should cover at minimum:

PURCHASE_MOTIVATION
UNDERLYING_UNMET_OUTCOME
PRE_PURCHASE_OBJECTION
POST_PURCHASE_FRICTION
ASPIRATION
NOISE

If the existing taxonomy already has better equivalent concepts, reuse/extend it.

===========================================================
PHASE 3 — LLM SEMANTIC CLASSIFIER
===========================================================

Use an LLM where semantic interpretation is actually necessary.

The classifier should determine:

"What role does this signal play in the customer's relationship with the product/category?"

It must distinguish examples such as:

"I don't know what marketing strategy to follow."

→ PURCHASE_MOTIVATION / UNDERLYING_UNMET_OUTCOME

from:

"I am worried this won't produce ROI."

→ PRE_PURCHASE_OBJECTION

from:

"My refund took three weeks."

→ POST_PURCHASE_FRICTION

from:

"I want to become the market leader."

→ ASPIRATION

The LLM MUST NOT create new pains.

It may classify ONLY evidence-backed signals supplied to it.

Require structured output.

Every classification must preserve the original pain/signal ID and evidence IDs.

===========================================================
PHASE 4 — DETERMINISTIC CLASSIFICATION JUDGE
===========================================================

The LLM classifier is NOT the final authority.

Add deterministic/code enforcement after classification.

Verify:

- pain ID exists
- evidence IDs exist
- evidence belongs to the same tenant/campaign/run where required
- no fabricated evidence
- classification refers to the supplied signal
- source type is preserved
- direct customer evidence is distinguished from competitor messaging
- product capability is respected
- no new pain statement was invented
- no unsupported causal relationship was introduced

If classification cannot be trusted:

do not silently promote the pain.

Return review/degraded/unknown according to existing architecture.

===========================================================
PHASE 5 — EVIDENCE ROLE MATTERS
===========================================================

Frequency alone must NOT determine strategic importance.

Examples:

100 refund complaints

does NOT automatically mean:

refund = primary purchase motivation.

It may mean:

strong POST_PURCHASE_FRICTION.

Similarly:

competitor marketing copy repeating "save time"

does not automatically prove customers experience "lack of time" as their strongest pain.

Ranking must consider:

- classification
- evidence quality
- evidence diversity
- prevalence
- causal depth
- purchase relevance
- product fit
- strategic role

===========================================================
PHASE 6 — CONTEXTUAL DOMINANCE
===========================================================

THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT REQUIREMENT.

Replace global pain dominance with CONTEXTUAL DOMINANCE.

There must NOT be:

ONE PRIMARY PAIN
→ forced into every engine.

Instead:

Each engine receives the authoritative Pain Registry and selects/ranks only pains ELIGIBLE for that engine's responsibility.

Conceptual routing:

POSITIONING:
Prefer:
- PURCHASE_MOTIVATION
- UNDERLYING_UNMET_OUTCOME

Avoid as core:
- POST_PURCHASE_FRICTION

DIFFERENTIATION:
Prefer pains revealing:
- unmet category outcome
- failure of existing alternatives
- meaningful customer dissatisfaction the product can uniquely solve

MECHANISM:
Use pains/root causes that explain WHY the primary strategic problem persists and that the product mechanism genuinely resolves.

OFFER:
Prefer:
- PURCHASE_MOTIVATION
- UNDERLYING_UNMET_OUTCOME

May additionally use:
- PRE_PURCHASE_OBJECTION

Example:

Core Offer:
solve fragmented tools / lack of strategic direction

Risk reversal:
address ROI uncertainty

Do NOT make refund support the core Offer unless evidence/classification genuinely proves it is the purchase motivation.

AWARENESS:
Use pains appropriate to the customer's awareness state.

FUNNEL:
Prefer:
- PRE_PURCHASE_OBJECTION
- conversion barriers
- trust barriers
- uncertainty

PERSUASION:
Use:
- motivations
- objections
- proof requirements
- desired outcomes

RETENTION:
Prefer:
- POST_PURCHASE_FRICTION
- support issues
- onboarding issues
- refund/cancellation friction
- continued-value problems

CONTENT:
May use multiple pains across different content pieces.

But each content angle/item must preserve which pain ID it addresses.

===========================================================
PHASE 7 — NO PAIN MERGING
===========================================================

Add explicit enforcement:

ROUTE PAINS.
DO NOT BLEND PAINS.

Engines must NOT merge unrelated pains merely to satisfy:

- alignment guards
- CEL
- grounding
- System Control
- Offer validation
- dominance requirements

For example, do NOT transform:

Pain A:
"Businesses are overwhelmed by fragmented marketing tools."

Pain B:
"Customers complain about slow refunds."

into:

"Businesses suffer from fragmented marketing refund complexity."

That is synthetic strategic nonsense.

Each pain keeps:

- its ID
- its meaning
- its classification
- its evidence
- its strategic role

An engine may use multiple pains ONLY when their roles are explicit.

Example:

Offer:

corePainId = pain_001
objectionPainId = pain_004

NOT:

mergedPain = pain_001 + pain_004

===========================================================
PHASE 8 — DO NOT DROP SECONDARY PAINS
===========================================================

Contextual dominance does NOT mean deleting lower-ranked pains.

All validated pains remain available.

The system should distinguish:

CORE
SUPPORTING
OBJECTION
RISK
RETENTION
CONTENT
NOT_ELIGIBLE_FOR_THIS_ENGINE

Example:

pain_001:
fragmented tools / lack of direction
role for Offer = CORE

pain_002:
ROI uncertainty
role for Offer = OBJECTION

pain_003:
refund delays
role for Offer = NOT_CORE
role for Retention = CORE/SUPPORTING

Nothing gets lost.

It gets ROUTED.

===========================================================
PHASE 9 — ENGINE DOMINANCE CONTRACT
===========================================================

Every relevant engine must receive explicit dominance instructions.

Conceptually:

"You are receiving an authoritative classified Pain Registry.

Do not reinterpret pain classification.

Do not invent pains.

Do not merge unrelated pains.

Select the highest-ranked pain eligible for YOUR engine role.

Use secondary pains only according to their allowed strategic roles.

Preserve pain IDs in your output.

If you choose a lower-ranked eligible pain over a higher-ranked one, provide an explicit evidence-backed reason."

The LLM does not get to silently override classification.

===========================================================
PHASE 10 — DETERMINISTIC ENGINE JUDGE
===========================================================

After each relevant engine runs, code must validate its pain usage.

Verify:

1. Referenced pain IDs exist.

2. Classification matches authoritative registry.

3. Pain is allowed for this engine.

4. Core pain is eligible for core use.

5. Highest-priority eligible pain was used OR a valid explicit reason exists.

6. Secondary pains are used only in allowed roles.

7. No unrelated pains were merged.

8. No pain was invented.

9. Evidence lineage remains intact.

10. Product capability can genuinely address the selected pain.

If an engine violates the contract:

reject/regenerate using precise feedback.

Do not silently repair the text after generation.

===========================================================
PHASE 11 — PRODUCT FIT ENFORCEMENT
===========================================================

A pain can be:

REAL

but still:

NOT SOLVABLE BY THIS PRODUCT.

That distinction is mandatory.

Example:

A customer's accounting problem may be real and heavily evidenced.

If the product is an AI marketing strategy platform:

that does not make accounting an eligible core strategic promise.

Therefore core strategy eligibility requires:

REAL CUSTOMER PAIN
+
CORRECT CLASSIFICATION
+
STRATEGIC RELEVANCE
+
PRODUCT CAPABILITY FIT
