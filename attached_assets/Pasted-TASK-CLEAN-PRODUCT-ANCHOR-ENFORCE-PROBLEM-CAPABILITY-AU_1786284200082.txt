GLOBAL AUTHORITY MODEL
===========================================================

This is the permanent system rule:

PAIN REGISTRY
= authority for WHAT PROBLEM EXISTS
and which problem is eligible for each engine.

PRODUCT ANCHOR / PRODUCT IDENTITY
= authority for WHAT THE PRODUCT CAN ACTUALLY DO.

ENGINE
= authority only for HOW an eligible problem can be addressed using validated product capabilities.

JUDGE
= verifies that the engine respected all three boundaries.

In simple form:

Pain Registry:
WHAT PROBLEM?

Product Anchor:
WHAT CAN THE PRODUCT DO?

Engine:
HOW CAN A REAL CAPABILITY ADDRESS THIS ELIGIBLE PROBLEM?

Judge:
DID THE ENGINE PRESERVE BOTH AUTHORITIES WITHOUT INVENTING OR MERGING?

These namespaces must remain separate:

PROBLEM_NAMESPACE
→ Pain Registry

CAPABILITY_NAMESPACE
→ Product Anchor

SYNTHESIS_NAMESPACE
→ Engine output

No namespace may silently create authoritative truth inside another namespace.
