TASK — CHANGE CROSS-SIGNAL EVIDENCE PHILOSOPHY TO USE AVYRON'S EXISTING CUSTOMER EVIDENCE

We completed the forensic audit of `degraded_no_decisions`.

The audit proved:

- The 30 current cross-signal decisions were correctly rejected under the CURRENT rules.
- `LinkInBio` and similar CTA labels are metadata leakage.
- Cross-posted identical competitor captions can create pseudo-corroboration.
- The current cross-signal layer effectively requires a `real` customer-origin source to validate market decisions.
- Reviews were missing in the audited campaign.
- However, the SAME campaign already has an authoritative Audience/Pain architecture with 13 approved evidence-backed pains.
- Those pains successfully powered Audience, Strategy Root, Positioning, Offer, and downstream strategy.
- That evidence currently travels through a parallel path and is NOT available to the cross-signal decision layer.

The current philosophy needs to change.

IMPORTANT:

Avyron targets startups and SMBs.

Many customers will enter competitors that:

- have no Trustpilot presence
- have no G2 presence
- have very few formal reviews
- are local businesses/startups
- have customer discussion mainly in comments/social channels

Therefore:

FORMAL REVIEWS MUST NOT BE A REQUIRED CONDITION FOR VALIDATED MARKET INTELLIGENCE.

Trustpilot, G2, Google Reviews, etc. may remain useful OPTIONAL sources.

They must NOT be architectural prerequisites.

===========================================================
NEW CORE PHILOSOPHY
===========================================================

The system should be:

CUSTOMER-EVIDENCE DRIVEN

NOT:

REVIEW-PLATFORM DRIVEN

A source is valuable because of WHAT the evidence represents, not because of WHICH WEBSITE it came from.

The system should reason in terms of semantic evidence roles:

CUSTOMER_ORIGIN_EVIDENCE
COMPETITOR_BEHAVIOR
COMPETITOR_MESSAGING
INFERRED_SYNTHESIS
METADATA
NOISE

NOT simply:

reviews = real
everything else = not real

2 — CUSTOMER EVIDENCE IS SOURCE-AGNOSTIC
===========================================================

A valid customer-origin signal may come from:

- Instagram comments
- TikTok comments
- YouTube comments if supported later
- Reddit/community discussions if supported
- Google Reviews
- Trustpilot
- G2
- Capterra
- app reviews
- testimonials where provenance is trustworthy
- other verified customer/user-origin sources

The PLATFORM is secondary.

The semantic origin is what matters.

Example:

Competitor Instagram post:

"Save 10 hours every week with our AI."

This is:

COMPETITOR_MESSAGING

NOT customer evidence.

Instagram comment:

"I tried three tools and still don't know what marketing strategy I should follow."

If provenance confirms this is a user/customer-origin comment, this may be:

CUSTOMER_ORIGIN_EVIDENCE

The fact that both came from Instagram does NOT make them the same evidence type.

===========================================================

A comment can qualify as customer-origin evidence only when provenance and content pass deterministic validation.

7 — DO NOT TURN PAINS THEMSELVES INTO FAKE RAW EVIDENCE
===========================================================

===========================================================
8 — INDEPENDENCE MATTERS
===========================================================

Validation should care about independent voices, not platforms.

Current bug:

same competitor posts identical caption on Instagram + TikTok
→ system may count 2 sources.

That is wrong.

Same normalized content
+
same competitor/entity
+
cross-posted platforms

should generally count as ONE independent voice.

Conversely:

three different customer comments from independent users
on the same platform

may represent THREE independent customer artifacts, subject to deduplication and provenance validation.

Therefore:

SOURCE DIVERSITY ≠ PLATFORM COUNT

Think in terms of:

independent evidence artifacts
independent authors/voices where available
independent competitors
semantic source roles
