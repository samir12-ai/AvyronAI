# P-6.7 Competitor Coverage Audit — Why Does Audience See Only One Competitor?

The previous investigation concluded that:

```
competitorCount = 1
```

This limits confidence to approximately **0.3933**, preventing `root_causes` from ever being generated.

However, the production campaign contains **10 configured competitors**.

This creates a major contradiction.

Do not implement fixes.

Do not modify thresholds.

Do not speculate.

Determine why the pipeline believes there is only one competitor.

Every conclusion must be supported by production data, code, execution traces, and database evidence.

---

# Mission

Trace the lifecycle of all competitors from campaign configuration to the Audience Engine.

Determine exactly where the other competitors disappear.

---

# Investigation

## 1. Campaign Configuration

For the investigated campaign:

Return:

* Total configured competitors
* Competitor IDs
* Competitor names
* Status
* Platform
* Enabled/Disabled

Confirm whether exactly 10 competitors exist.

* Which filters are applied?
* Which competitors survive those filters?

Show the exact competitor list entering the orchestrator.
