The audit proved that Avyron currently has strong systems for:

- evidence grounding
- pain eligibility

- absolute quality checks

But it lacks a reliable process for choosing the BEST strategic angle when multiple valid and eligible truths exist.

The current dominant pattern is:

eligible candidates
→ frequency/rank/score
→ sort

- frequency saturated the scoring formula

- array position committed the winner
- no serious alternatives were compared
- judges asked whether the winner was acceptable, not whether it was superior

- the candidate required a product capability Avyron does not possess

GLOBAL DOMINANCE CONTRACT
===========================================================

Every affected engine must receive this dominance rule:

Your task is not to select the highest-ranked, most frequent, first eligible, or first-present truth.

Your task is to:

1. Identify all serious evidence-grounded alternatives relevant to your engine responsibility.

2. Remove candidates that:
   - are not eligible for this engine
   - require unsupported semantic expansion
   - require unsupported product capabilities
   - do not resolve to authoritative evidence
   - contradict Product Identity
   - are merely high-frequency but strategically irrelevant

3. Compare the remaining serious candidates according to your engine’s responsibility.

4. Select a winner only if it is demonstrably stronger than its alternatives.

5. Preserve uncertainty when no candidate clearly dominates.

Frequency, rank, confidence, array order, and prevalence are evidence inputs.

They are NOT winner-selection authority.

PHASE 5 — SEMANTIC ENTAILMENT GUARD
===========================================================

Implement a semantic-entailment contract.

The question is:

Does the proposed strategic meaning follow from the source evidence?

Allowed:

Evidence:
"Owners feel overwhelmed by fragmented marketing tools."

Candidate:
"Replace fragmented tools with one evidence-grounded strategic direction."

Possible result:
ENTAILED or SUPPORTED, if Product Identity supports the solution.

Not allowed:

Evidence:
"Users value community and belonging."

Candidate:
"Founders require peers to validate their marketing strategy."

Result:
UNSUPPORTED_SEMANTIC_EXPANSION unless direct evidence establishes peer validation.

The guard must detect unsupported additions such as:

- new psychological need
- new buying motive
- new causal relationship
- new market problem
- new product capability
- new solution category

The LLM may rephrase.

It may not change the underlying meaning.

Use deterministic checks where possible and an LLM judge only where semantic reasoning is genuinely required.

The semantic judge must receive:

- original evidence
- normalized source meaning
- candidate meaning
- claimed transformation
- referenced capabilities
- required IDs

It must return structured results:

ENTAILED
PARTIALLY_ENTAILED
UNSUPPORTED_EXPANSION
CONTRADICTED

with exact unsupported additions.
