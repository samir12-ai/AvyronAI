P-4 Architecture — Strategic Reasoning Cards & Market Memory

The Watchtower is now complete.

Its responsibility ends after producing grounded Market Insights.

Do NOT add strategic reasoning inside the Watchtower.

Instead, build a new layer called:

Strategic Reasoning Layer

==================================================
Architecture
============

Watchtower

↓

Verified Market Insights

↓

Performance Loop Insights

↓

Business Context

↓

Historical Market Memory

↓

Strategic Reasoning Engine

↓

Reasoning Cards

The Reasoning Layer is responsible for connecting multiple intelligence sources into one coherent business interpretation.

==================================================

1. Inputs
   ==================================================

The Reasoning Engine must combine:

• Watchtower Market Insights

• Performance Loop outcomes

• Historical Performance Memory

• Historical Market Memory

• Company profile

• Competitor context

• Current business objectives

No raw posts.

No raw classifications.

Only verified outputs from upstream engines.

==================================================
2. Responsibilities
===================

The Reasoning Engine should answer:

Why does this matter?

Why now?

Who is affected?

What evidence supports this?

How does this compare with previous market behavior?

Has this happened before?

Is this temporary or recurring?

Is this relevant to this company?

What signals reinforce each other?

==================================================
3. Market Memory
================

Every validated Market Insight should be stored.

Store:

Timestamp

Campaign

Window

Signal groups

Dominant themes

Emerging themes

Declining themes

Market movement summary

Confidence

Evidence references

This creates a long-term market history.

==================================================
4. Historical Reasoning
=======================

The engine should detect recurring behaviors.

Examples:

"This market moved toward conversion messaging three times during the past twelve months."

"Educational messaging consistently returns after seasonal promotional campaigns."

"The current shift resembles the movement detected in March."

Historical comparisons must rely only on stored Market Memory.

==================================================
5. Reasoning Cards
==================

Generate customer-facing cards such as:

Market Direction

Market Momentum

Recurring Pattern

Strategic Context

Competitive Pressure

Evidence Summary

Confidence

Uncertainty

Each card must cite the evidence used.

==================================================
6. Grounding
============

Every card must reference:

Market Insight IDs

Performance Insight IDs

Historical Memory IDs

The engine must never invent evidence.

==================================================
7. Validation
=============

Run against the live MarketMindAI campaign.

Verify:

Historical memory updates correctly.

Reasoning references historical events.

Cards remain grounded.

No unsupported conclusions.

==================================================
8. Deliverables
===============

Return:

Architecture

Memory schema

Reasoning flow

Card definitions

Validation

Live campaign results

PASS / FAIL

PASS only if:

* Watchtower remains focused on market observation.
* Reasoning combines multiple intelligence layers.
* Historical Market Memory enables comparisons across time.
* Every card is fully evidence-based.
* No strategic recommendations are generated at this layer.
