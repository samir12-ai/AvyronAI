2. AI Responsibilities
======================

The AI should answer questions such as:

What overall market story do these signals tell?

Which signals appear connected?

Which changes appear independent?

What business behavior might explain the observed changes?

Are multiple semantic shifts reinforcing the same market direction?

Which observations are strongest?

Which observations remain uncertain?

The AI should synthesize.

It should NOT calculate.

==================

Avoid template outputs such as:

"Question hooks increased."

Instead produce reasoning similar to:

"The market appears to be moving toward more conversion-oriented messaging. While this convergence is statistically significant, the available evidence does not yet establish that these changes improved commercial outcomes."

Every statement must be supported by deterministic evidence.
