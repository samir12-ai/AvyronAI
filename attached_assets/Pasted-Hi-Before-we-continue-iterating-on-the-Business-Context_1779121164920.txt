Hi,

Before we continue iterating on the Business Context Layer (BCL), I want to pause and properly audit the actual data availability and engine dependencies across the system.

I suspect a large portion of the information the BCL needs may already exist inside the current pipeline, but not all of it is available at the same stage of orchestration.

Right now I do not want us to solve this by adding excessive manual user inputs.

Instead, I want a proper audit answering:

* what business-context signals already exist,
* where they exist,
* when they become available,
* which engines produce them,
* which stages depend on other stages,
* and what minimum viable business profile can be constructed progressively throughout orchestration.

Important:
The BCL should not assume all fields exist from the beginning.
It should support progressive enrichment.

Example:
Early-stage profile:

* industry
* business category
* basic buyer type
* website/service understanding
* high-level commercial lens

Later-stage enrichment:

* offer clarity
* ICP refinement
* positioning
* funnel bottlenecks
* pricing complexity
* objection patterns
* trust gaps
* activation friction
* retention signals

I want an audit of:

1. Existing data already available in the system
   Please inspect:

* scraped website data
* social/account bios
* reviews
* campaign/user inputs
* positioning outputs
* audience outputs
* offer engine outputs
* market intelligence outputs
* strategy outputs
* product DNA
* canonical snapshots
* signal maps
* orchestration context

For each:

* what fields exist,
* how reliable they are,
* when they become available,
* and whether they are usable for BCL construction.

2. Engine dependency timeline
   Map:

* which engines run first,
* which engines depend on later engines,
* what context exists before Offer Engine,
* what context only becomes available afterward,
* and how a progressive BCL should evolve across the orchestration lifecycle.

3. Progressive BCL design recommendation
   I want a proposed staged architecture:

Stage 1:
Minimal business understanding from early evidence only.

Stage 2:
Enriched profile after mid-pipeline engines.

Stage 3:
Final commercial lens before strategic synthesis / agents.

The key point:
BCL must not block on unavailable engines or unavailable fields.

Unknown fields should remain explicitly unknown — never hallucinated.

4. Missing data analysis
   Please identify:

* what critical business signals are truly missing,
* what can already be inferred safely,
* what should remain optional,
* and whether any additional user input is genuinely necessary.

5. Recommendation
   After the audit:
   Recommend the cleanest architecture for making the LLM understand the business context progressively without creating hard dependencies, hallucinated assumptions, or orchestration deadlocks.

Do not implement new architecture yet.
First perform the audit and produce the dependency/data map so we can decide the next step based on actual system reality rather than assumptions.
