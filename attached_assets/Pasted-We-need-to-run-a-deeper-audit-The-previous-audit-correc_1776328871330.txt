We need to run a deeper audit. The previous audit correctly identified rule gaps, but it is still operating at the wrong level.

The issue is NOT only missing hard rules.

The real issue is that engines are not interpreting signals correctly, and decisions are being made without a shared logical understanding of the data.

Right now:

* Signals are extracted correctly
* But they are not translated into meaning
* And that meaning is not enforced across engines

This is why we see:

* Valid data → invalid strategy
* Correct signals → contradictory decisions
* High scores → logically broken outputs

---

### Objective

Audit how signals are interpreted, transformed into meaning, and propagated across engines.

We are NOT looking for:

* More scoring fixes
* More penalties
* More surface-level rule additions

We ARE looking for:

* How decisions are actually formed from signals
* Where logical understanding breaks
* Why engines can contradict each other even when data is correct

---

### Required Audit Scope

For EACH engine:

#### 1. Signal Interpretation Layer

* What raw signals does the engine receive?
* How are these signals interpreted internally?
* Are signals just passed through, or transformed into meaning?

Example:
If awareness = unaware:

* Does the engine understand this means low trust, low urgency, low clarity?
* Or does it just store "unaware" as a label?

---

#### 2. Meaning Construction

* Does the engine derive internal constraints from signals?
* Or does it operate independently using generic logic?

We need to know:

* How the engine translates data into decisions
* Whether it builds reasoning or just applies scoring

---

#### 3. Constraint Propagation

* Once a signal is interpreted, how does it affect downstream behavior?
* Are constraints shared across engines?

Example:
If audience is unaware:

* Does funnel automatically shift to education-first?
* Does persuasion reduce direct conversion pressure?
* Does offer adapt positioning?

Or do engines ignore this and decide independently?

---

#### 4. Dependency Awareness

* Does the engine validate upstream quality before using it?
* Can it detect when upstream data is unreliable or weak?

Example:

* Positioning confidence = 0.20
* Why do downstream engines still fully trust and use it?

We need full tracing of:

* Input confidence vs output confidence
* Whether confidence inheritance exists or not

---

#### 5. Contradiction Origin Tracing

For the current campaign, trace EXACTLY where these contradictions are created:

* Audience = product_aware → Channel Selection behaves as unaware
* Positioning = 0.20 → Mechanism = 0.95
* Offer not aligned with pains → still accepted
* Statistical validation = rejected → system proceeds
* Funnel trust = 1.0 → Awareness says no trust path

For EACH case:

* Identify the engine where the logic breaks
* Identify WHY it breaks (missing interpretation, missing propagation, or wrong assumption)

---

### Critical Requirement

We need a clear answer to this:

Does the system currently:
A) Interpret signals into meaning and enforce them across engines
B) Or simply pass signals and let each engine decide independently

Be explicit. No assumptions.

Deliver a structured report including:

  * Signal → Meaning → Decision flow
* Clear identification of missing interpretation layers
* Evidence of whether constraint propagation exists or not
* Root cause mapping for all major contradictions
* Identification of where “understanding” is missing
