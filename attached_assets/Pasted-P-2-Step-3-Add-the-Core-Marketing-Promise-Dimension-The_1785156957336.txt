---

Background

During verification, the current classifier correctly identifies:

* Hook
* Hook Archetype
* Narrative
* CTA Type
* Offer Type
* Emotional Trigger
* Awareness Stage
* Positioning Style
* Content Format Intent

However, one critical marketing dimension is still missing.

The classifier understands HOW the post communicates.

It still does not capture WHAT the post is fundamentally promising.

That missing dimension is the Core Marketing Promise.

---

Goal

Extend the existing classifier with one additional structured dimension.

Core Marketing Promise

This should represent the main customer value proposition communicated by the post.

Examples:

* Save Time
* Save Money
* Better Quality
* Premium Experience
* Family Experience
* Convenience
* Trust & Reliability
* Social Status
* Exclusivity
* Better Taste
* Better Health
* Entertainment
* Community
* Personal Growth
* Simplicity

If no clear promise exists,

return UNKNOWN.

Do NOT hallucinate.

---

Important distinction

This is NOT:

* Hook
* Angle wording
* Emotional Trigger
* CTA

Instead it answers:

"What is the customer actually being promised?"

Examples:

"Fast delivery"

Core Marketing Promise

= Convenience
