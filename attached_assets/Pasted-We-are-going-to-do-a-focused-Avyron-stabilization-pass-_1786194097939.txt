===========================================================
WHY WE ARE DOING THIS
===========================================================

The recent MarketMindAI real runs exposed a structural problem.

Different engines use "confidence" to mean different things.

Examples already discovered:

- Market Intelligence confidence can represent evidence/data quality.
- Positioning has engineConfidence and confidenceScore with different semantics.
- Territory confidence can represent territory maturity/evidence strength.
- Mechanism had strong local structural quality (~0.95) but was capped to ~0.20 through an upstream inherited-confidence ceiling.
- Downstream engines could inherit/carry low confidence.
- System Control then compared heterogeneous values through CONFIDENCE_SPREAD_EXCESSIVE.

This produced a false block:

MI ≈ 0.92
Mechanism ≈ 0.20
spread ≈ 0.72
threshold = 0.50
→ BLOCK

But the 0.20 did NOT mean Mechanism itself was poor.

Therefore we no longer want one generic cross-engine "confidence" doctrine controlling the strategic pipeline.

Also consider UX:

A user seeing "Confidence 70%" cannot know whether that means:

- probability the strategy succeeds
- AI certainty
- evidence quality
- data completeness
- grounding quality
- territory maturity
- structural output quality

If the number cannot be interpreted consistently, it should not be presented as a universal strategic truth.
