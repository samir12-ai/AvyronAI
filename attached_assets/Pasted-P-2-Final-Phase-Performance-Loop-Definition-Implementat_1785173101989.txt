The goal of this task is to define exactly what the Performance Loop is, audit what already exists, complete only the missing core logic, and run one real controlled experiment using the MarketMindAI campaign.

==================================================

1. Product Definition
   ==================================================

The Performance Loop is not a generic analytics dashboard.

Its purpose is:

To learn which marketing decisions increase the client’s sales, preserve successful decisions, reject weak decisions, and improve the next strategy cycle using both client results and market intelligence.

The primary success metric is:

SALES

Sales are entered manually by the user for each reporting period.

Other metrics may help explain the result, but they must not replace sales as the final success criterion.

==========================

Audit which of these inputs already exist and where they are stored.

Required inputs:

A. Client outcome inputs

* Sales
* Reporting period start date
* Reporting period end date

Supporting inputs where available:

* Ad spend
* Number of published posts

B. Client strategy inputs

The Loop must know what Avyron recommended during the tested period, including where available:

* Recommended emotional triggers
* Recommended positioning styles
* Recommended CTAs

C. Actual client execution

The Loop must determine what the client actually published or executed during the same period.

Where user post classifications already exist, consume them.

Where they do not exist, document the exact gap.

Do not invent or infer execution data that is not stored.

D. Market context

Consume existing intelligence from:

* Market Intelligence
* Watchtower
* Pipeline Boss
* competitor_post_classifications

Use market context only to explain performance and improve decisions.

Market activity is not the success metric.

==================================================
3. Core Learning Logic
======================

The Loop must compare:

Recommended strategy
vs.
Actual client execution
vs.
Sales outcome
vs.
Market changes

The Loop should produce evidence-based conclusions such as:

* A recommended angle was applied and sales increased.
* A recommended angle was applied and sales decreased.
* Sales increased, but the client did not follow the recommendation.
* Sales decreased while the client followed the plan.
* Results are inconclusive because several variables changed simultaneously.
* A market shift may have affected the result.
* There is insufficient evidence to make a causal claim.

Do not automatically attribute every sales change to content.

The system must distinguish:

* correlation
* probable contribution
* strong evidence
* insufficient evidence

==================================================
4. Strategic Memory
===================

Audit the existing approved/rejected angle and learning persistence.

The Loop must maintain durable memory for each campaign.

For every tested strategic decision, store or verify storage of:

* decision or angle
* tested period
* whether it was actually executed
* sales before
* sales after
* supporting context
* confidence
* verdict

Allowed verdicts:

* WINNER
* LOSER
* INCONCLUSIVE
* NOT_EXECUTED
* NEEDS_MORE_DATA

Rules:

* Preserve WINNER decisions for future strategy generation.
* Avoid repeating LOSER decisions without new evidence.
* Do not mark a decision as LOSER when it was not executed.
* Do not mark a decision as WINNER from only vanity metrics.
* Do not overwrite historical results when a new period is submitted.

==================================================
5. Decision Output
==================

The Performance Loop output must be a strategic decision, not only a metric summary.

Every completed cycle should answer:

1. What happened to sales?
2. Which planned decisions were actually executed?
3. Which decisions likely contributed?
4. What should be preserved?
5. What should be rejected?
6. What remains uncertain?
7. What should change in the next cycle?

The next-cycle recommendation may adjust only evidence-supported variables, such as:

* Hooks
* Primary angles
* Core marketing promises
* Emotional triggers
* Positioning
* CTA
* Content mix
* Offer framing

Do not allow the Loop to rewrite the entire strategy from one data point.

8. Required Test Scenarios
==========================

Verify at least these scenarios through unit, integration, or controlled test data:

Scenario 1:
Recommendation executed and sales increase.

Expected:
Likely WINNER, subject to confidence and context.

Scenario 2:
Recommendation executed and sales decrease.

Expected:
Likely LOSER or NEEDS_MORE_DATA depending on evidence.

Scenario 3:
Recommendation not executed and sales decrease.

Expected:
NOT_EXECUTED.
The recommendation must not be blamed.

Scenario 4:
Sales increase but several strategic variables changed together.

Expected:
INCONCLUSIVE or probable contributors.
Do not claim certainty.

Scenario 5:
Likes and reach increase but sales decrease.

Expected:
Commercial failure.
Do not classify as success.

Scenario 6:
Reach decreases but sales increase.

Expected:
Commercial success, with explanation.

Scenario 7:
No sales input submitted.

Expected:
Loop remains incomplete and does not fabricate a verdict.
