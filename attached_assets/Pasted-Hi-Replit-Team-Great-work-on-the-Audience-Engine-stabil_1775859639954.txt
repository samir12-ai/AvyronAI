# 🔴 1. CRITICAL — Must Be Done First (Before Any New Work)

## Fix Confidence Calculation Denominator

Currently:

* `computeCalibratedConfidence()` is calibrated for **3 source types**

With the upcoming additions (comments, captions, website, TikTok, reviews), this will break confidence scoring.

### Required:

* Replace hardcoded `/3` with:

  * either `/5`, or
  * a constant (preferred): `MAX_EXPECTED_SOURCE_TYPES = 5`

### Why this is critical:

If not fixed:

* confidence will be inflated
* signals will appear stronger than they are
* decision quality will degrade silently

👉 This must be implemented and verified **before proceeding to any ingestion or schema changes**
