P-3 Enhancement — Watchtower Distribution Intelligence Layer

The semantic Watchtower is now operational and verified.

Do not replace the existing semantic change detection.

Do not redesign the current promotion pipeline.

Instead, extend Watchtower so it fully utilizes competitor_post_classifications as a market intelligence dataset rather than only a semantic change detector.

==================================================
Background
==========

Watchtower currently detects semantic shifts correctly.

Examples:

Promise Shift

Emotion Shift

Narrative Shift

CTA Shift

Awareness Shift

etc.

However, competitor_post_classifications contains significantly richer information than change detection alone.

The platform should also understand the structure of the market itself.

==================================================
Goal
====

Create a Distribution Intelligence Layer above the existing semantic change detection.

Watchtower should answer questions such as:

What dominates the market?

What is growing?

What is declining?

What patterns are emerging?

What patterns are disappearing?

without introducing additional LLM analysis.

Use existing competitor_post_classifications only.
