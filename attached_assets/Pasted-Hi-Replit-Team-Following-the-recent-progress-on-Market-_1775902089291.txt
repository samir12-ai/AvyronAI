## 🔴 TikTok Specific — Validation Layer Requirement

TikTok should not be treated as raw input only.

It must act as a **validation engine**, meaning:

> Only content that actually performs should influence decisions.

### Required Logic

Before using any TikTok content as a signal, we need:

#### 1. Success Qualification Layer

The system must determine whether a video is:

* High-performing
* Mid-performing
* Low-performing

Based on **relative performance**, not absolute metrics.

#### 2. Relative Performance (Key Principle)

Performance should be evaluated against the creator’s baseline:

* views vs average views
* engagement rate vs average
* shares/comments ratios

Not:

❌ “This video has high views → it’s good”
But:
✅ “This video outperformed this creator’s baseline”

#### 3. Composite Success Score

A weighted score combining:

* views (relative)
* engagement rate
* shares
* comments
* recency

#### 4. Signal Usage Rules

* High-performing → strong validation signal
* Mid-performing → weak signal
* Low-performing → excluded or used as negative signal

And most importantly:

> Repetition only matters if it appears in high-performing content
