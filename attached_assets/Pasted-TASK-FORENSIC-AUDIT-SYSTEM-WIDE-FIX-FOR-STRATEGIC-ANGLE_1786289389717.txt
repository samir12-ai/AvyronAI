===========================================================
KNOWN FAILURE CASE
===========================================================

Audience contained several strong, grounded strategic truths, including themes such as:

- lack of strategic direction
- generic/non-personalized marketing advice
- need for clarity grounded in actual market evidence
- need to adapt as competitors/market conditions change

But Positioning centered on:

Community / Peer Validation

because community-related evidence was frequent / highly ranked / eligible for Positioning.

This was strategically wrong.

The evidence did NOT establish:

"business owners need peers to validate their strategy"

and Avyron does NOT provide peer strategy validation.

The engine transformed a broad community/belonging signal into a new strategic meaning:

community
→ peer validation
→ peer-validated strategy

That semantic expansion was unsupported.

So the problem is NOT simply:

"wrong pain classification"

and NOT simply:

"wrong Product Anchor"

Those protections already improved.

The deeper question is:

HOW DOES EACH ENGINE CHOOSE THE STRATEGIC ANGLE IT WILL BUILD AROUND WHEN MULTIPLE VALID, ELIGIBLE, EVIDENCE-GROUNDED TRUTHS ARE AVAILABLE?

We currently do not trust that selection behavior.

===========================================================
PHASE 7 — REQUIRED PROPERTIES OF THE FIX
===========================================================

Whatever architecture you choose must guarantee:

1. ELIGIBILITY IS NOT WINNER SELECTION

"Allowed for Positioning" does not mean "best Positioning."

2. FREQUENCY IS NOT STRATEGIC IMPORTANCE

Frequency may contribute evidence strength but cannot alone choose an angle.

3. NO FIRST-ITEM / ARRAY-ORDER BIAS

Candidate order must not determine strategy.

4. ALTERNATIVES MUST BE CONSIDERED

When multiple materially different credible angles exist, the system must compare them before committing.

5. SOURCE MEANING MUST BE PRESERVED

community/belonging cannot become peer strategy validation unless evidence actually establishes that relationship.

6. PRODUCT REALITY MUST BE PRESERVED

No candidate may depend on a capability Avyron does not possess.

7. ENGINE RESPONSIBILITY MUST MATTER

The winning truth for Retention does not have to be the winning truth for Positioning.

8. STRONG AUDIENCE INTELLIGENCE MUST NOT DISAPPEAR

If strategically important ideas exist in:

- Audience pains
- segments
- desires
- objections
- causal evidence
- behaviors
- AEL/root causes

the selection architecture must not accidentally ignore them because only one reduced list is consumed downstream.

9. JUDGES MUST VERIFY SELECTION QUALITY

Where appropriate, the judge must know the serious alternatives and verify why the winner is superior for that engine's responsibility.

10. NO HARDCODING THIS CASE

Do not encode:

"community is weak"

or:

"strategic direction is strong"

The same architecture must work for a business where community genuinely IS the strongest buying driver and the product genuinely provides it.
