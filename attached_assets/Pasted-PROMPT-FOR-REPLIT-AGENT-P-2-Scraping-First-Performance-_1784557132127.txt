# Phase 3 — Deterministic Content Scoring

The scoring truth is produced by code.

## A. Honest cohorts

Comparisons must respect:

* same account
* same platform
* comparable post format where necessary
* compatible metric availability
* comparable post age / checkpoint maturity
* time-bounded baseline

Do not directly mix Instagram and TikTok.

Do not compare a 24-hour observation to a 7-day observation without explicit maturity handling.

## B. Checkpoint maturity

Expose one maturity state per scored post:

* `MATURE_7D`
* `PROVISIONAL_72H`
* `EARLY_24H`
* `OBSERVED_LATE`
* `IMMATURE`
* `UNKNOWN`

Only one observation per post may enter one scoring run.

## C. Public metric registry

Create a config-driven registry based only on metrics verified in Phase 1.

Possible metrics include:

* views growth
* likes per view
* comments per view
* visible engagement per view
* likes per follower
* comments per follower
* engagement velocity

Use only metrics with honest numerator and denominator availability.

A missing denominator returns UNAVAILABLE.

Do not label public metrics as reach, saves, or conversions unless that exact metric is actually available.

## D. Self-baseline

All content scoring compares the user with the same user.

The baseline must be:

* account-specific
* platform-specific
* maturity-specific
* time-bounded
* based on comparable posts
* versioned
* timestamped

No global benchmark.

Exclude the evaluated post from its own baseline where appropriate.

## E. Scoring dimensions

Score only supported lineage dimensions:

* hook style
* content angle
* content type

For each dimension value, persist:

* sample size
* included post IDs
* snapshot IDs
* maturity
* primary metric
* baseline
* measured result
* absolute delta
* relative delta
* consistency
* outlier concentration
* confounders
* confidence
* verdict
* scorer version

Verdicts:

* `WINNING`
* `NEUTRAL`
* `UNDERPERFORMING`
* `TESTING`
* `UNKNOWN`

Rules:

* Fewer than 3 comparable posts: `TESTING`.
* No usable metric: `UNKNOWN`.
* One viral post cannot create `WINNING`.
* One weak post cannot create `UNDERPERFORMING`.
* Mixed evidence becomes `NEUTRAL` or `TESTING`.
* Thresholds are config-driven.
* Thresholds must not be lowered to manufacture a result.

# Phase 4 — Weekly Business Outcome Scoring

The weekly manual inputs are a separate truth layer.

## A. Persist and normalize weekly outcomes

For each week, use:

* leads
* qualified
* booked
* paying

Calculate honestly where denominators exist:

* lead-to-qualified rate
* qualified-to-booked rate
* booked-to-paying rate
* lead-to-paying rate
* week-over-week deltas
* trailing self-baselines

A missing number remains unavailable.

Do not treat a missing week as zero activity.

## B. Business verdict

Produce one weekly business verdict:

* `WORKING`
* `DRIFTING`
* `UNKNOWN`

Rules must be config-driven and based on the business’s own baseline.

Example logic direction:

* `WORKING`: meaningful improvement in downstream outcomes with sufficient data.
* `DRIFTING`: deterioration across meaningful funnel stages.
* `UNKNOWN`: missing inputs, too little history, or conflicting movements.

Do not inherit any old unsafe Q1 logic without auditing its semantics.

## C. Attribution confidence

Content performance and weekly business outcomes must not be presented as causal unless the evidence supports it.

Use explicit confidence levels:

* `DIRECT`
* `SUPPORTED`
* `CORRELATED`
* `UNKNOWN`

Examples:

* `DIRECT`: the user supplied a source or post/campaign link for the lead.
* `SUPPORTED`: strong campaign/channel association, but not post-level proof.
* `CORRELATED`: outcomes improved during the same period as content performance.
* `UNKNOWN`: no reliable relationship can be inferred.

Without direct source input, the system may say:

“Qualified leads increased during the same week.”

It may not say:

“This angle generated the leads.”

## D. Optional minimal source input

If the existing weekly form supports safe extension, add optional fields:

* main lead source
* related campaign
* related post URL
* channel
* attribution known / unknown

Do not make these required for weekly submission.

The UI must remain simple for a solo SMB owner.

# Phase 5 — Contracted AI Interpretation

## A. AI input

## B. AI responsibilities

AI may:

* explain what the factual results mean
* identify semantic similarities between hooks or angles
* identify possible confounding factors
* explain the relationship between public attention and business outcomes
* generate one controlled next experiment
* state what evidence is still needed

AI must separate:

1. Proven result.
2. Observed correlation.
3. Hypothesis.
4. Unknown.

## C. Performance Interpretation Contract

Every interpretation must include:

### What is proven

* content verdict
* business verdict
* metrics
* sample size
* maturity
* evidence references

### What is correlated

* timing relationship between content and weekly outcomes
* explicit attribution confidence

### What is only a hypothesis

* possible reasons the hook or angle performed
* clearly labeled as hypothesis

### What could be confounding the result

* format
* timing
* follower growth
* outlier post
* hook-angle overlap
* organic versus paid traffic
* referral or offline source
* missing attribution
* insufficient maturity

### What to test next

One controlled experiment:

* one main variable changed
* relevant constants preserved
* target platform
* target hook or angle
* measurement checkpoint
* metric to evaluate
* business metric to observe
* what outcome strengthens or weakens the hypothesis

### Why it is specific to this campaign

The interpretation must name the actual product, audience, posts, hooks, angles, and evidence.

It must not apply unchanged to a generic competitor.

## D. Judge

Reuse and extend the existing interchangeability and evidence judgment infrastructure.

Reject AI output if it:

* changes the deterministic verdict
* presents correlation as causation
* invents private metrics
* invents customer behavior
* omits evidence
* ignores insufficient sample size
* recommends scaling a `TESTING` dimension as proven
* hides confounders
* gives generic marketing advice
* produces a fixed recommendation template
* claims that content caused sales without adequate attribution
* references unavailable metrics

Required rejection log:

`PERFORMANCE_INTERPRETATION_REJECTED`

If retries fail:

* deterministic results stay visible
* interpretation becomes visibly unavailable
* no fallback template appears
* log `PERFORMANCE_INTERPRETATION_UNAVAILABLE`
