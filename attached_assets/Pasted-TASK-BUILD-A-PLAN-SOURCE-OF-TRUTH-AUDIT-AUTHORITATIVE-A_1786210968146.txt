B7 — CONTEXTUAL DOMINANCE
===========================================================

THIS IS CRITICAL.

There must NOT be one global primary pain forced into every engine.

Each engine must select:

THE HIGHEST-PRIORITY ELIGIBLE PAIN FOR ITS OWN JOB.

Contextual dominance.

POSITIONING:

Prefer:
PURCHASE_MOTIVATION
UNDERLYING_UNMET_OUTCOME

Do not normally allow:
POST_PURCHASE_FRICTION as core positioning.

DIFFERENTIATION:

Prefer pains showing:
- failure of alternatives
- unmet category outcome
- strategically important customer dissatisfaction
- product-solvable gap

MECHANISM:

Use root causes behind the selected core strategic problem that the product can actually resolve.

OFFER:

Core:
PURCHASE_MOTIVATION / UNDERLYING_UNMET_OUTCOME

Supporting:
PRE_PURCHASE_OBJECTION

Example:

CORE:
"Businesses are overwhelmed by fragmented tools and lack strategic direction."

OBJECTION:
"They fear paying for another tool without clear ROI."

Offer may use BOTH.

But with separate roles.

FUNNEL:

Prefer:
PRE_PURCHASE_OBJECTION
trust barriers
conversion barriers
uncertainty

PERSUASION:

Use:
motivations
objections
proof requirements
desired outcomes

RETENTION:

Prefer:
POST_PURCHASE_FRICTION

Examples:
refund
support
onboarding
access
cancellation
continued-value problems
