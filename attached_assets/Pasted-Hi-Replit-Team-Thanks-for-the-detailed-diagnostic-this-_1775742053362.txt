* Pull recent entries from `strategy_decisions`

* **Observation** (what changed)
* **Why it matters** (context / comparison)

Example:

> I’ve detected a shift in your campaign.
> Reels are underperforming vs market (1.8% vs 3.9% save rate).
> Suggested: shift 1 slot → carousel next week.

**Guidelines:**

* 2–4 insights max (no spam)
* Separate messages (not one block)
* Tone: strategist, not system logs
* Agent should initiate (not wait for user prompt)
