## For EACH Engine — Required Breakdown

### 1. Internal Pipeline (Step-by-Step)

Describe the full internal flow:

- How input is processed
- What transformations happen
- What intermediate representations are created
- What sequence of logic is applied

Example (structure, not content):
- Step 1: parse input → extract signals
- Step 2: cluster/group
- Step 3: generate hypotheses
- Step 4: filter/prune
- Step 5: scoring
- Step 6: output construction

I want the real internal stages, not high-level descriptions.

---

### 2. Generation Logic (LLM / Heuristic Behavior)

For any generation step:

- What exactly is being generated?
- Based on what?
- What constraints are applied?
- What is allowed vs not allowed?

Specifically:
- Does it generate freely then filter?
- Or is generation constrained from the start?

---

### 3. Evidence Usage

- How does the engine use signals/data?
- At what step are signals injected?
- Are outputs strictly derived from signals or partially inferred?

Explicitly answer:
> Can this engine produce outputs that are not directly traceable to input signals?

If yes:
- where and why?

---

### 4. Decision Points

List all internal decisions:

- branching logic
- thresholds
- selection criteria
- prioritization rules

Example:
- why choose one segment over another?
- why select one territory?
- why reject alternatives?

---

### 5. Scoring & Confidence Logic

- How is confidence calculated internally?
- What affects it?
- Is it based on:
  - structure?
  - data?
  - heuristics?
  - penalties?

Clarify:
- what lowers confidence
- what increases it

---

### 6. Failure Modes

For each engine:

- When does it fail logically (not system block)?
- What are typical weak outputs?
- Where does it “hallucinate” or over-expand?

---

### 7. Constraint Enforcement

- What constraints are enforced inside the engine itself?
- Are they:
  - hard constraints
  - soft (post-processing penalties)

---

### 8. Output Construction

- How final outputs are assembled
- What parts are:
  - directly derived
  - synthesized
  - inferred
