Wanted to share an honest status update on the PBCL workstream and a corrected proposal for next steps.

**What actually landed (PBCL-12)**

While tracing the Awareness engine failure, I identified a positional-argument bug at the orchestrator → Awareness call boundary. The BCL context object was being passed into the wrong argument slot, which meant BCL Stage 2 was never firing — and the resulting crash was masking itself as a DEPTH_FAILED signal.

The fix was a single-line correction in server/orchestrator/index.ts (~L2287). No changes were made to the Awareness engine itself or to awareness-depth-interpreter.ts.

Verified outcome (Fifth Measurement):
- BCL_INJECTIONS: 0 → 2
- Stage 2 calls: 0 → 2
- Awareness output: json_null → 13/30 (b2b_saas), 12/30 (local_services)

**What is still failing**

The depthScore gate is not yet passing. Post-fix, Awareness runs without crashing, but still hits DEPTH_FAILED at depthScore=0.1 against a threshold of ≥0.5. This is because the synthetic seeds do not produce enough AEL evidence to lift the score. As a result, Funnel, Persuasion, and Integrity are still returning json_null — meaning we have no judge scores for those engines yet, only nulls.

**Proposed next steps**

Given that engines 7–10 have not produced scoreable output yet, propagating BCL to those engines now would be propagating on a guess. The data-driven propagation priority the team wants does not exist until those engines actually run.

The correct sequence is:

1. PBCL-13: Strengthen synthetic seed coverage so AEL produces sufficient evidence to lift depthScore above the 0.5 gate.
2. PBCL-15: Run the full 3-industry v5 audit — this is the Sixth Measurement, and the first point where Funnel/Persuasion/Integrity will have real judge scores.
3. PBCL-18: Use those scores to identify which engines most need BCL grounding, then propagate engine-by-engine with a measurement pass between each.

Running the audit again now would reproduce the Fifth Measurement unchanged. The next meaningful run is after PBCL-13 lands.

This sequencing is consistent with B1 doctrine (Truthfulness over confidence) and the §13-criterion-2 acceptance threshold — no rollout claim until ≥+2 lift on ≥2/3 industries with no regression.
