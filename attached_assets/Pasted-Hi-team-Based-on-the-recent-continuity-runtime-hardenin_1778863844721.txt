PRIORITY #1 — OPERATOR DASHBOARDS
=================================

This is now the highest priority.

We need deep operational visibility into the live system before expanding beta exposure.

* retry loops
* scraper health
* queue depth
* scheduler cadence
* supervisor state
* dead/degraded chains
* zombie watchdog interventions
* heartbeat freshness
* ingestion health
* failed source visibility
* stale-source visibility
* worker/runtime health
* AI spend
* provider consumption
* retry amplification
* orchestration pressure
* runtime degradation indicators

The system is now operationally complex enough that we need live visibility into runtime behavior at all times.

If the system degrades, operators must immediately understand:

* what failed
* where
* why
* severity
* affected systems
* recovery path

No silent operational ambiguity should remain.

==================================================
PRIORITY #2 — SPEND CAPS & BETA KILL SWITCH
===========================================

Before broader beta rollout, I want strict operational safety controls.

Please implement:

* per-account AI spend caps
* scraping/provider usage caps
* retry-spend protection
* emergency beta kill switch
* emergency orchestration pause
* emergency scraping pause
* emergency provider disable controls
* runaway-cost protection
* abuse/throttling protection
* beta admission gating

The system must be able to:

* stop safely
* throttle safely
* degrade safely
* recover safely

without operational chaos or uncontrolled spending.

==================================================
PRIORITY #3 — USER-FACING CONFIDENCE & DEGRADED-DATA VISIBILITY
===============================================================

Once operator visibility and safety controls are stable, I want stronger trust surfaces exposed to end users.

Please make visible:

* plan confidence
* degraded-data states
* stale-data warnings
* missing-data warnings
* low-confidence recommendations
* source-health impact

Users should understand:

* confidence level
* evidence quality
* degradation impact
* when the system is uncertain

This is a core trust requirement for beta.
